## About the Author

**Nik Bear Brown** teaches AI, data science, and applied mathematics at Northeastern University. He has taught math at every level from college algebra through advanced computational biology, at institutions including Northeastern, UCLA, Santa Monica College, and the Art Institutes.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from UCLA. He is the founder of [Humanitarians AI](https://www.humanitarians.ai/) and the author of the *with LLMs* textbook series, which includes companion volumes in calculus, statistics, and contemporary mathematics.

He works in Boston. [nikbearbrown.com](https://www.nikbearbrown.com) · [irreducibly.xyz](https://irreducibly.xyz) · [skepticism.ai](https://www.skepticism.ai)
